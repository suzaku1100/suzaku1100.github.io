TintFall Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 8, 2026

TintFall ("the app") is a free, offline puzzle game for iPhone published by Eri Munakata. This policy explains what data is processed when you play, by whom, and the choices you have.

What we collect ourselves

Nothing. TintFall has no account system, no analytics of our own, and no servers. Your puzzle progress, settings, and streaks are stored only on your device and are deleted when you delete the app. Progress never leaves your device and we cannot access it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

TintFall is funded by ads served through Google AdMob (Google LLC). When the app requests or shows an ad, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process data, including:

  • Device identifiers — including the advertising identifier (IDFA) if you allow tracking, or a non-personalized context if you do not
  • Coarse location inferred from your IP address
  • Advertising data such as which ads were shown and interacted with
  • Usage and diagnostics information related to ad delivery

Google uses this data to serve and measure ads, prevent fraud, and — where permitted — personalize ads. See Google's Privacy Policy and AdMob's partner information for details.

App Tracking Transparency

On first launch, iOS asks whether TintFall may track you across apps and websites. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", the advertising identifier is not used and ads become non-personalized. The game is identical either way — nothing is locked, slowed, or hidden. Apple's SKAdNetwork framework may still be used for privacy-preserving ad attribution.

Regional note (United Kingdom, Switzerland, EEA)

TintFall does not currently include a certified consent-management platform (CMP). In regions where Google requires certified consent collection (including the UK, Switzerland, and EEA countries), Google may decline to serve ads; the game remains fully functional. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may still transmit basic device data when it initializes. If you are in one of these regions and have questions or objections regarding this processing, contact us at the address below — you can also simply play offline, in which case no ad requests are made at all.

Children

TintFall is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children; the app contains no chat, no social features, and no in-app purchases.

Data deletion

Deleting the app removes all locally stored progress and settings. For data processed by Google, see the Google links above; you can also reset your device's advertising identifier in iOS Settings at any time.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
