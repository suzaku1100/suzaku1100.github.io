How to play

  • Tap a vessel to select it, then tap another vessel to pour.
  • Only the top color moves, and it lands only on a matching color or an empty vessel. If there is not enough room, the rest stays behind.
  • Finish the level by filling every vessel with a single color.

Frequently asked questions

Is every level actually solvable?
Yes — provably. Each of the 120 levels ships with a solution found by our solver before release, and the "Par" shown in-game is that machine-verified minimum move count.

I'm stuck. What are my options?
Undo is unlimited and free. Restart deals the identical board, so you can try a different line. You also get three free hints every day; watching a short rewarded ad adds a few more, and that is always optional.

What is the ad policy?
Ads never appear during a level, on a retry, or at app launch. Between-level ads are hard-capped: none until you have cleared ten levels, at most one per three clears, and never twice within two minutes. If you see behavior that breaks these rules, please report it — that is a bug.

Does TintFall work offline?
Yes, fully. Ads simply do not appear while offline.

How does the Daily Pour work?
Every calendar day has one puzzle, the same for every player. Clear it to mark the day on your calendar and extend your streak. Replays are welcome but only the first clear counts.

Can I move my progress to a new phone?
Progress is stored only on your device and is not synced or backed up by us, so it cannot be transferred. This is part of how TintFall stays account-free and offline.

I can't tell some colors apart.
Turn on Color symbols in Settings — every ink gets a distinct shape mark. TintFall also honors the iOS "Differentiate Without Color" setting on first launch.
