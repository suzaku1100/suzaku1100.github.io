TintFall

A calm color-sort puzzle for iPhone.

Pour inks between vessels until every vessel holds a single color. No lives, no timers, no noise — just the quiet satisfaction of things settling into place.

Why TintFall is different

  • Every level is machine-verified. All 120 levels ship with a solver-proven solution and a proven minimum move count — no impossible walls.
  • Ads never interrupt a level. Interstitials are hard-capped and only ever appear between levels. Rewarded ads are always your choice.
  • Honest retries. Restart always deals the identical board. Undo is free and unlimited.
  • 100% offline. Play on a flight or in a tunnel — nothing is locked behind a connection.
  • Colorblind-friendly. Optional shape marks on every ink.
  • Quiet by design. No music, no blaring audio — soft haptics only.
