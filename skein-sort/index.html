Skein Sort: Yarn Color Puzzle

A calm, honest color-sorting puzzle for iPhone. Pull yarn from either end of the skein, wind three of a kind onto your spools, and knit your blanket one square at a time.

  • Every level machine-verified solvable — no ads or purchases needed
  • Progress saved after every move
  • No lives, no timers, fully playable offline
  • Colorblind-friendly stitch symbols
