Skein Sort — Support

How to play

Where do I pull from?
A skein of yarn has two ends. Tap either end to wind the next bobbin onto the rack. The whole strand is visible from the start — plan ahead.
How do patches work?
Each bobbin winds onto a spool of its color. Three of a kind knits a patch and frees the spool for reuse.
What is the basket?
From level 13 you can stash a bobbin from either end in the basket and wind it later. It is the key to untangling tricky strands.
I'm stuck. Is my level broken?
No level is broken: every level ships with a machine-checked solution that needs no ads, boosters, or purchases. Use Undo freely, or restart the level at no cost.

Your progress is never lost

Skein Sort saves after every single move. If a call interrupts you or the app is closed mid-level, you continue exactly where you left off.

Our ad policy

  • Every level is solvable without watching any ad.
  • Interstitials appear only between levels, never during play, never after a failed attempt, and never in your first session — at most one per three cleared levels and never more than one per two minutes.
  • Rewarded ads are strictly optional (hints and level skips).
  • Ads are always muted. There is no ad banner during gameplay.

If an ad ever misbehaves (won't close, plays sound, or shows deceptive content), please email a screenshot — we can block the offending ad source.
