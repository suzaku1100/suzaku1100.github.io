Privacy Policy

Skein Sort: Yarn Color Puzzle · Effective date: August 9, 2026

Skein Sort is a fully offline puzzle game. The app itself has no account system, no server, and collects no personal information. Your game progress is stored only on your device.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Skein Sort is free and shows ads through Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). To serve and measure ads, Google may collect data such as device identifiers (for example the advertising identifier, IDFA, where permitted), coarse location derived from your IP address, ad interaction data, and crash/diagnostic data. This data is processed by Google under its own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses data from partner apps

App Tracking Transparency

On first launch, iOS asks whether Skein Sort may track you across other companies' apps and websites. If you choose Allow, ads may be personalized using your device's advertising identifier. If you choose Ask App Not to Track, you will still see ads, but they will not be personalized in that way. The game is fully playable either way.

United Kingdom, Switzerland and EEA note

This version of Skein Sort does not include a certified consent management platform. As a result, Google may choose not to serve ads in the United Kingdom, Switzerland, or EEA countries; the game remains fully playable without ads. Please note that the advertising SDK may still transmit basic device data when it initializes. If you have questions or objections, contact us at the address below.

Data retention and deletion

All game data (progress, settings) lives only on your device. Deleting the app deletes this data. We hold no copy of it.

Children

Skein Sort is a general-audience app and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new effective date.
