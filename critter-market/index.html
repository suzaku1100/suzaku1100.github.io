Critter Market

A cozy idle shop in the woods — for iPhone.

Stock your tiny market stand with acorns, berries, honey and more, then go live your day. Forest critters wander in to shop — even while the app is closed. Tap each visitor to say hello, earn coins, and collect all 18 woodland characters in your album.

Made to be gentle

  • Honest ads. Tap “Collect” and you will never be shown an ad. Boosts are optional, clearly labeled, and never required.
  • No ads at all in your first session.
  • Provably fair. Every critter can be met without watching a single ad — machine-verified before release.
  • Works offline. No account, no sign-in, no push notifications.
  • A 2–3 minute check-in. Restock, greet, done.
