Critter Market — for iPhone

How to play

  1. Put goods on a shelf from the Market book.
  2. Wait — critters visit on their own schedule, even while the app is closed.
  3. Tap a visitor to greet them: you earn coins and they join your album.
  4. Unlock new goods and add shelves to attract rarer visitors.

Frequently asked questions

Do I have to watch ads?
No. Tapping “Collect” never shows an ad, and every critter — including the rare ones — can be met without watching a single ad. Optional boosts (double coins, inviting a visitor) are the only places ads appear by choice, plus an occasional capped ad after browsing the Market book (never in your first session).

Does it work offline?
Yes. The whole game runs on your device. Without a connection you simply see no ads.

Where is my save?
On your device only. Deleting the app deletes your shop and album. There is no account and no cloud sync in this version.

A rare critter won't show up.
Check its card in the Album — the “Loves:” line tells you which goods attract it. Keep that item stocked; the longer it stays on the shelf, the sooner the critter is guaranteed to appear.

I found a bug.
Please email us with your iPhone model and iOS version — we read everything.
