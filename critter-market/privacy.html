Privacy Policy

Critter Market — Last updated: August 8, 2026

Critter Market is a casual game published by an independent developer. The game itself runs entirely on your device: it requires no account, collects no personal information of its own, and works offline. Your shop, coins, album progress and settings are stored only on your device and are deleted when you delete the app.

Advertising

Critter Market shows ads served by Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process data from your device, including:

  • Device identifiers (such as the advertising identifier, IDFA, where you have allowed tracking)
  • Coarse location inferred from your IP address
  • Advertising interaction data (ads viewed, taps)
  • App interaction and diagnostic data

How Google uses this data is described in Google's Privacy Policy and AdMob's data usage documentation.

App Tracking Transparency

On first launch, iOS asks whether Critter Market may track you across apps and websites. If you decline, the advertising identifier is not shared with Google and you will see non-personalized ads (or no ads). The full game is playable either way — every critter can be met without watching a single ad, and tapping “Collect” never shows an ad.

SKAdNetwork

The app includes Apple's SKAdNetwork identifiers, which let ad networks measure ad conversions in a privacy-preserving way without identifying you.

Regional note (United Kingdom, Switzerland, Iceland, Liechtenstein, Norway)

Critter Market does not currently include a certified consent-management platform (CMP). In regions where Google requires certified consent collection, Google will generally not serve ads to this app — you will simply see no ads there. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may still transmit device data (such as IP address and device information) when it initializes. We plan to add a consent-management flow in a future update. Critter Market is not distributed in the 27 EU member states.

Children

Critter Market is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

  • You can change the tracking permission at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Deleting the app deletes all locally stored game data.
