Frequently asked questions

Does the game work offline?

Yes — completely. Every level, upgrade, and hint works with no connection. Without a connection no ad requests are made at all.

Is anything locked behind ads or payments?

No. There are no in-app purchases, and every one of the 120 levels is verified beatable before release without watching a single ad and without buying any upgrade. Optional ads only double salvage you already earned or reveal the field-notes hint, and a free partial hint unlocks by itself after a few defeats on the same level.

How often do full-screen ads appear?

Never during play. A brief interstitial can appear between levels, at most once every three results and never more than once every three minutes — and never in your first session.

Where is my progress stored?

Only on your device. There is no account and no server. Deleting the app deletes your progress; keeping it safe across devices is not yet supported (planned).

I found a level that seems impossible.

Every level ships with a machine-verified winning plan (that plan is exactly what the hint shows). If a level still feels wrong, email us the level number — we take these reports seriously.

How do I reset my progress?

Delete and reinstall the app. We keep nothing on any server, so a reinstall is a true fresh start.
