Ash Haven

The defense puzzle where what you see is what you play. Draw barricades with one finger, ignite the wave, and keep the last campfire burning while the horde claws at your walls.

How it plays

  • Study the gates, then sketch up to three walls — every line costs ink.
  • Survive the night: zombies chew through weak timber and flood the gaps.
  • Spend salvage on lasting upgrades and push into six ever-harder districts.

Why it's different

  • Every level is beatable without ads — each of the 120 levels ships with a machine-verified winning plan.
  • 100% offline — no account, no server, no energy timers.
  • No paywall — there are no in-app purchases at all.
  • Honest ads — optional bonuses only, strict frequency caps, never during play.
