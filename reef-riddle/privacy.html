Privacy Policy

Reef Riddle — Last updated: August 8, 2026

Reef Riddle is a match-3 puzzle game published by an independent developer. The game itself runs entirely on your device: it requires no account, collects no personal information of its own, and works offline. Your level progress, coins, aquarium and settings are stored only on your device and are deleted when you delete the app.

Advertising

Reef Riddle shows ads served by Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process data from your device, including:

  • Device identifiers (such as the advertising identifier, IDFA, where you have allowed tracking)
  • Coarse location inferred from your IP address
  • Advertising interaction data (ads viewed, taps)
  • App interaction and diagnostic data

How Google uses this data is described in Google's Privacy Policy and AdMob's data usage documentation.

App Tracking Transparency

On first launch, iOS asks whether Reef Riddle may track you across apps and websites. If you decline, the advertising identifier is not shared with Google and you will see non-personalized ads (or no ads). The full game is playable either way — every board can be completed without watching a single ad.

Regional note (United Kingdom, Switzerland, EEA)

Reef Riddle does not currently include a certified consent-management platform (CMP). In regions where Google requires certified consent collection (including the UK, Switzerland, and EEA countries), Google may decline to serve ads; the game remains fully functional. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may still transmit basic device data when it initializes. If you are in one of these regions and have questions or objections regarding this processing, contact us at the address below and we will help — you can also simply play offline, in which case no ad requests are made.

Children

Reef Riddle is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children; the app contains no chat, no social features, and no in-app purchases.

Data retention and deletion

We (the developer) do not operate any servers and do not store your data. Progress and settings live only in your device storage: deleting the app deletes them. For data processed by Google for advertising, see Google My Ad Center and Google's Privacy Policy.
