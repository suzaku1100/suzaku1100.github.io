Frequently asked questions

Is a level impossible?

No — and we can back that up. Before release, an automated solver plays every level, and each board you're dealt comes from a pool of deals the solver has already beaten. If you're stuck, leave the board alone for a few seconds and a suggested move will light up, or try building a Surge (match four) or a Prism (match five in a row).

Do I need an internet connection?

No. All 80 levels and your aquarium are stored on your device. Offline, the game simply shows no ads.

Why did my board reshuffle?

When no legal swap exists, Reef Riddle automatically reshuffles the tiles for free — you never lose a move or get stuck.

How do ads work?

Reef Riddle is completely free and contains no purchases. A few ads keep it that way, under hard rules: never during a level, never after a loss, never in your first session, at most one after every third win, and at least three minutes apart. Bonus ads (extra moves, coins) play only when you tap them. See Settings → Fair Play & Ads in the app.

My progress disappeared

Progress is stored on the device. Deleting the app deletes progress; there is no cloud backup in this version. iOS device backups (iCloud/Finder) do include it.
