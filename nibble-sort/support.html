Nibble Sort — Support

How to play

  • Every level is a snack drawn in colored pieces. Jars arrive on a shelf belt — the whole belt is visible from the start, and you can take any jar inside the front window.
  • Tap a jar to place it on the counter. It packs away pieces of its color that are exposed on the outside of the picture, from the edge inward.
  • A full jar leaves and frees its counter spot. If every spot is stuck waiting on buried colors, the kitchen jams.
  • Undo is unlimited and free. Retries never shuffle the level — the same plan will always play out the same way.

Our ad policy, in plain words

Nibble Sort is free and shows a few ads. We hold ourselves to hard rules, built into the app and verified before every release:

  • Every level is solvable without watching a single ad — machine-verified by a solver before shipping.
  • No interstitial ads in your first session, at most one per three completed levels, and at least 120 seconds apart.
  • Ads never interrupt play: never mid-level, never after a failed run, never when you return to the app.
  • Rewarded ads (hints, level skip) are strictly optional and only appear when an ad is actually available.
  • No coins, no boosters, no energy system, and nothing to buy — the app contains no in-app purchases at all.

FAQ

Does it work offline? Yes — completely. In airplane mode you simply see no ads.

I can't beat a level — is it broken? Every shipped level has a machine-verified solution. Check which colors are buried under others and park their jars on the counter early; unlimited undo makes experimenting free. After two failed runs, an optional ad-powered skip appears if an ad is available.

Colorblind support? Every color is paired with a shape on both tiles and jars (on by default; see Settings).

How do I reset my progress? Delete and reinstall the app — all data lives on your device only.
