Curio Merge

A cozy antique shop merge puzzle for iPhone.

Bring a quiet little curio shop back to life, one merge at a time. Merge loose cogs into grandfather clocks, fill collectors' orders, and restore all twelve corners of the shop — fully offline, with no energy walls and nothing to buy, ever.

  • No energy walls — merging and delivering are always free
  • No in-app purchases, no pop-ups, no paywalls
  • Plays fully offline, small download
  • Colorblind-friendly: every color has its own symbol
