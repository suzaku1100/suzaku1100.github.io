Curio Merge — Support

How to play

  • Tap a generator on the shelf below the board to produce a piece (costs 1 energy).
  • Drag two matching pieces together to merge them into the next tier. Seven tiers per chain.
  • Deliver collectors' orders for coins and experience — delivering is always free.
  • Restore the shop: spend coins on each of the twelve fixtures, unlock new generators and more board space.

About energy

Energy only powers the generators. Merging, delivering, and selling never cost energy, so you always have a move. Energy refills over time (+1 every minute), fully refills when you level up, and some orders grant bonus energy. You can also trade coins for energy, or watch an optional rewarded ad. There is nothing to buy — Curio Merge has no in-app purchases at all.

Our ad policy

Curio Merge is free because of ads, and we keep them honest:

  • Every chapter is machine-verified completable without watching a single ad.
  • Rewarded ads are always optional and clearly labeled.
  • Interstitials appear only at natural breaks, hard-capped (never more than one per six deliveries or three minutes, never in your first session, never mid-move).
  • No banner ever appears on the game board, and ads play muted.

FAQ

Does it work offline? Yes — everything except ads works in airplane mode.

Where is my save? On your device. There are no accounts or servers. Deleting the app deletes your progress.

The board is full — am I stuck? Never permanently: merge pieces down, deliver an order, or sell a piece you don't need (tap it, then Sell).

Colorblind support? Every chain pairs its color with a unique symbol, on every piece, always.
