Privacy Policy

Curio Merge — Last updated: August 9, 2026

Curio Merge is a free iOS game supported by ads. This policy explains what data is involved and how it is handled. The short version: the game itself collects nothing and works fully offline; the only data leaving your device is what the advertising SDK needs to serve ads.

Data the game itself stores

Your progress (board, orders, restorations, settings) is stored only on your device. There are no accounts, no sign-in, and no game servers. Deleting the app deletes this data. We cannot access, restore, or transfer it.

Advertising

Curio Merge shows ads through Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process data such as:

  • Device identifiers (including the advertising identifier, IDFA, if you allow tracking)
  • Coarse location inferred from your IP address
  • Advertising interaction data and app usage data
  • Crash and diagnostics data

This data is collected by Google under its own terms. See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses data in ads.

App Tracking Transparency (ATT)

On first launch, iOS asks whether Curio Merge may track you across apps. If you choose Ask App Not to Track, the IDFA is not shared and you will see non-personalized ads. The game is fully playable either way — every chapter can be completed without watching a single ad.

For players in the UK, Switzerland, and the EEA

Curio Merge does not yet include a certified consent management platform (CMP). As a result, Google may not serve ads in these regions, and the game simply runs without ads there. Please note the SDK may still contact Google servers during initialization. A CMP is planned for a future update.

Children

Curio Merge is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children; the game itself collects no personal data from anyone.

Your choices

  • Reset or withdraw tracking permission: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking
  • Limit ad personalization: Settings > Privacy & Security > Apple Advertising, and Google Ads Settings
  • Delete all game data: delete the app
