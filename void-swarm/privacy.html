Privacy Policy

Void Swarm — Last updated: August 5, 2026

Void Swarm is an arcade survival game published by an independent developer. The game itself runs entirely on your device: it requires no account, collects no personal information of its own, and is fully playable offline. Your progress (salvage, upgrades, records, and any saved run) is stored only on your device and is deleted when you delete the app.

Advertising

Void Swarm shows ads served by Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process data from your device, including:

  • Device identifiers (such as the advertising identifier, IDFA, where you have allowed tracking)
  • Coarse location inferred from your IP address
  • Advertising interaction data (ads viewed, taps)
  • App interaction and diagnostic data

How Google uses this data is described in Google's Privacy Policy and AdMob's data usage documentation.

Our advertising principles

Ads never interrupt a run. Full-screen ads appear only between runs, with strict frequency limits. Bonus ads (revive, double salvage) are always optional, clearly labeled, and never required to progress — every sector can be cleared without watching a single ad.

App Tracking Transparency

On first launch, iOS asks whether Void Swarm may track you across apps and websites. If you decline, the advertising identifier is not shared with Google and you will see non-personalized ads (or no ads). The full game works the same either way.

Regional note (United Kingdom, Switzerland, EEA)

Void Swarm does not currently include a certified consent-management platform (CMP). In regions where Google requires certified consent collection (including the UK, Switzerland, and EEA countries), Google may decline to serve ads; the game remains fully functional. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may still transmit basic device data when it initializes. If you are in one of these regions and have questions or objections regarding this processing, contact us at the address below and we will help — you can also simply play offline, in which case no ad requests are made at all.

Children

Void Swarm is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app does not opt in to any child-directed advertising program.

Your choices

  • Change tracking permission any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Play offline — no ad requests are made without a connection, and the entire game works.
  • Delete the app to remove all locally stored progress.
