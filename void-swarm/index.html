Void Swarm

Neon space survivors. One drone, ten thousand machines, and a promise: your run is never interrupted.

Pilot a lone salvage drone through the Verge — a dead sector overrun by self-replicating machines. Your weapons fire on their own; you steer, dodge, and decide how your build grows.

Honest by design

  • No forced ads during runs. Ever. Bonus ads are always optional and clearly labeled.
  • No paywalls, no energy timers, no gacha — everything is earned in play.
  • Fully offline: play on a plane, in a tunnel, anywhere.
  • Put your phone down mid-run and resume exactly where you left off.
  • Every sector is beatable — verified by automated simulation before release.
