Frequently asked questions

Does the game work offline?

Yes — completely. Every sector, ship, and upgrade works in airplane mode. Without a connection you simply see no ads.

I had to quit mid-run. Is my run gone?

No. When Void Swarm goes to the background during a run, the run is saved automatically. Reopen the app and tap Resume Run on the home screen to continue exactly where you left off.

Are ads required?

Never. Runs are never interrupted by ads, and every sector is beatable without watching any. Optional ads exist only as bonuses: one emergency restart per run, and a salvage doubler on the results screen. If no ad is available, those buttons simply do not appear — nothing is lost.

I watched a bonus ad but something went wrong.

Void Swarm grants the bonus even when an ad fails to play back correctly — if you tapped the button, you get the reward. If you ever see otherwise, that is a bug: please email us.

Can I transfer my progress to a new phone?

Progress is stored only on your device in this version, so it cannot be transferred and is deleted with the app. Cloud backup is under consideration for a future update.

A sector feels impossible.

Every sector is verified beatable by automated simulation before release — but you may need more permanent upgrades first. Spend salvage in the Hangar (Hull and Calibration first), and try holding fewer, deeper weapon upgrades over collecting all four slots.
