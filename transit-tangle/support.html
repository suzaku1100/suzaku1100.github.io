Transit Tangle — Support

Transit Tangle is a calm bus-depot logic puzzle. Untangle the depot, dock each bus, and board every waiting passenger.

How to play

  • Tap a bus to send it to the dock — it can only leave when the lane ahead of it is clear, and only when the dock is free.
  • Matching passengers board automatically. A docked bus picks up waiting passengers of its color and symbol, and departs when every seat is filled.
  • Tap the front passenger to move them to a bench seat so the passengers behind them can board first. Bench seats are limited.
  • If the terminal jams up, use Undo or Restart — retries are always free, and the level layout never changes behind your back.

Our ad policy

We want Transit Tangle to be the honest one in its genre:

  • Every level is solvable without watching a single ad. Each shipped level is machine-verified to have a solution using only free moves.
  • Full-screen ads appear only occasionally between levels — never on a failed attempt, never on launch, and never more often than once every couple of minutes.
  • Rewarded ads are strictly optional (a hint, or skipping a level after repeated fails).
  • Ads are muted by default and never play sound over your music.
  • The game is fully playable offline. Playing offline simply means no ads.

FAQ

My progress disappeared. Progress is stored on your device only. Deleting the app deletes progress; reinstalling starts fresh. There is no account or cloud save in this version.

A level seems impossible. Every level has a verified solution without ads or boosters. Try benching the front passenger earlier, or dispatching buses in a different order — the Undo button is unlimited.

I saw no ads at all. That can happen (for example, offline or in some regions). Nothing is broken — the full game works without them.

The colors are hard to tell apart. Settings → "Colorblind symbols" keeps a distinct shape on every bus and passenger, so color is never the only cue. It is on by default.
