Transit Tangle

A calm bus depot logic puzzle for iPhone.

The depot is jammed. Send each bus out in the right order, dock it at the terminal, and board every waiting passenger — colors and symbols must match, bench seats are scarce, and the whole queue is visible from the start. Pure planning, no luck, no timers.

  • 60 hand-tuned levels, each machine-verified to be solvable without ads or boosters
  • Fully playable offline — no account, no energy system, no in-app purchases
  • Colorblind-friendly: every color is paired with a shape
  • Respectful ads with hard frequency caps
